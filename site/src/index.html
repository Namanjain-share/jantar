why it exists

Claude stops to ask. The tab it is waiting in sits behind eleven others.

  1. 01

    A dozen sessions in a dozen tabs. Which are working, which are done, and which are waiting on you?

  2. 02

    A permission prompt, a plan or a question stops a session until you answer it. If you are away from the Mac, it waits.

  3. 03

    With several Claude accounts, each 5-hour window fills at its own pace. You find out when a session stalls.

Jantar puts every session on one board, on the Mac and on your phone, and lets you answer from either.

jantar open

See every session. Answer the ones waiting on you.

The board lists every Claude Code session in Terminal.app, the ones that need you first. Each row says what it waits on: the question, the plan or the command. Open one and answer with the same options the Mac shows.

Every screen on this page is synthetic example data.

cards
With Claude Code's hooks installed (opt-in: jantar hooks install --apply), a permission prompt, a question or a plan becomes a card that answers through Claude Code itself. The hooks fail open: with Jantar stopped, Claude behaves as usual.
hold
Rows that save a rule or switch to a mode that doesn't ask need a 2-second hold.
send
A prompt counts as sent only when the session's transcript shows it arrived. If it doesn't, the phone says Not delivered and offers Retry.
attach
Photos and files from the phone go to the Mac, and Claude reads them from there. Photos are drawn again as JPEG, which drops their location data. Up to 10 files a prompt.
start
Start a new session in any folder you allow, or resume the last one. The sheet shows the exact command line before you hold to start. It needs both jantar launch on at the Mac and a phone granted launch: jantar devices grant <device> launch.

jantar controls · on by default

Without the hooks, Jantar reads the dialog off the screen. It types only while that dialog is still there.

A session that started before the hooks, or a subagent asking, still waits on a dialog in Terminal. The phone shows that dialog's question and Claude's own options as buttons.

Before and after it types

  1. Reads the screen again, under the session's lock, and compares it with what you tapped.
  2. Checks the dialog once more in the same AppleScript run that types, and types nothing if it changed.
  3. Sends only arrow keys; Terminal adds the one Return. Never a digit, Tab or Shift+Tab.
  4. Reads the screen until the dialog has closed or changed.

A screen Jantar can't turn into buttons gets a keypad: ↓, ↑ and Enter, sent in one write and only while the lines you counted on are unchanged. Rows it can't answer safely stay greyed, with one line saying why. Show on Mac is on every dock.

jantar meetings on · Chrome extension

Your Meet and Teams calls, kept on your Mac. Captions and a summary. No recording.

A Chrome extension keeps the call's live captions, the words Google or Microsoft already show you. After the call, one Claude run writes the summary, the decisions and the action items.

Keeps

  • Caption lines with the speaker and the time, from when you press Start notes.
  • A summary, decisions and action items. One tap puts an item in a project's todos.
  • The screenshots you click: the camera button, Option+Shift+S or the toolbar. Not yet seen working on a real call.

Never

  • Audio or video, or a bot in the call.
  • Anything before you press Start notes, beyond whether you are in a call and the page title.
  • Your screenshots, off the Mac. Only the transcript text goes to Claude, for the summary, through Claude Code on your account.

Chrome 120 or newer, Meet and Teams on the web. Loaded unpacked for now; it is not in the Chrome Web Store yet. Screenshots have not yet reached Jantar on a real call; captions and the summary have (the Meetings checks). Captions show only on your screen, so say at the start of the call that you keep notes.

privacy and security

A phone that can approve can run commands. So Jantar is reachable by as little as possible.

A paired phone can

  • See the board, the transcripts and what each session waits on.
  • Answer cards and terminal dialogs, and send prompts and files.
  • Start sessions, once the Mac turns launching on and grants it that (jantar devices grant).

No phone can

  • Turn a feature on, add a folder or grant itself a scope. Every switch is set at the Mac.
  • Start a session with a flag that isn't on a fixed list. There is no free-text flag.
  • Read or write a session without being paired. Over the network, every /v1 route but the pairing claim needs a paired device.
  • A dialog answer is typed only if the dialog is still exactly what you tapped, checked in the same AppleScript run that types it. A key pressed at the Mac in those few milliseconds can still come first: what compare-and-type can't close.
  • Treat a paired phone like your Mac's keyboard. Anything running as you on the Mac can reach Jantar's local sockets too, and could pair itself as a device; SECURITY.md says what that means.
  • Logs carry no prompt text by default. Life, Intel and Meetings text never goes into events, logs or audit rows.
  • Every test runs against a synthetic Mac: a fake Terminal, claude and cswap. The dialog and privacy suites record every keystroke Jantar would type. What has been tried on a real Mac is listed, with dates.

In the cloned, built repo, try it read-only first, with a throwaway state folder:

export JANTAR_HOME=/tmp/jantar-try
node packages/daemon/bin/jantard.mjs \
  --read-only --no-terminal --port 7491

Try it without touching anything

What doesn't work yet.

An early release, tested on a synthetic Mac and tried on a real one. These are the limits today.

terminals
Terminal.app on the Mac. With Fleet (v0.6, off until jantar fleet on), Linux servers with Claude in tmux, WSL2 and other Macs join the Mac's board; parts are not yet checked on real servers. iTerm2 and Ghostty later.
sleep
Medicine and reminder pings come from the daemon, so they need the Mac awake. With Fleet, so does reaching a server.
dialogs
Some dialog rows stay at the Mac until their real-Mac check passes.
meetings
Screenshots from the extension have not yet worked on a real call, and the extension is loaded unpacked: it is not in the Chrome Web Store.
accounts
Jantar does not move sessions between accounts, and sends no Telegram messages or Mac banners.
safari
Safari may refuse the daemon's cookie on plain http://127.0.0.1. On the Mac, use Chrome for jantar open, or your front door's HTTPS address.

macOS · Terminal.app · Node 22.13 or newer

One line, or clone and build. Then pair your phone.

You need

  • A Mac with Terminal.app (the only terminal so far)
  • Node.js 22.13 or newer, and pnpm 10
  • Claude Code, signed in (one account is enough)
  • For the phone: Tailscale (recommended) or a Cloudflare Tunnel
  • Optional: cswap for several Claude accounts; Chrome 120 or newer for Meetings

In one line

It checks for macOS, Node and pnpm, clones release v{{version}} into ~/jantar, builds it and runs jantar init, then asks before jantar install. No sudo. Read the script first. To see its plan with nothing changed, end the line with bash -s -- --dry-run instead of bash.

Or step by step

The alias lasts for this Terminal window. To keep the jantar command, put the CLI on your PATH, for example with cd packages/cli && pnpm link --global.

Approval cards

jantar hooks install --apply

Opt-in. A dry run first shows the change to Claude's settings.json; --apply asks, keeps a backup, and the hooks fail open.

Your phone

jantar remote setup

Tailscale Serve, or a Cloudflare Tunnel with Access. Open the link on the phone and Add to Home Screen; on iPhone that is needed for notifications.

Nothing changed

Try it read-only

With a throwaway state folder: no typing, no AppleScript, nothing written outside it.

before you install

Questions worth asking first.

>Do I need cswap?

No. One Claude login is enough, and Jantar finds it. cswap adds usage bars, several accounts and the primer.

>Can I run it on my server?

Beside your Mac, yes. Fleet (v0.6, off by default) pairs Linux servers with Claude in tmux, WSL2 and other Macs with the Mac, which stays the hub: the phone reaches them through it, so it must stay awake. Native Windows is a preview that shows sessions and their cards only, and parts are not yet checked on real servers. See servers.

>Do I need an API key?

No. Jantar uses Claude Code's own login. Its own Claude runs (Rewrite, Ask Claude, Ask, the primer, meeting summaries) run claude, or cswap run, with any API key or token taken out of the environment, so they use your subscription login.

>Is my data sent anywhere?

Not to us: there is no Jantar server and no telemetry. Your phone reaches your Mac through your own tailnet or tunnel. What goes to Anthropic is what Claude Code sends anyway, plus the text of Jantar's own Claude runs when you turn them on. Notifications go through Apple's or Google's push service, encrypted to your device. A meeting's screenshots never leave the Mac.

>Can a phone turn a feature on or widen its own reach?

No. If a lost or borrowed phone could turn on Files or starting sessions, pairing would protect much less. Every switch, folder, scope grant and the programs the daemon runs are set with the jantar command on the Mac. While a part is off, the phone shows the command that turns it on.

>What does Jantar type into my terminal?

Only what you send or answer, and the control keys you press (Shift+Tab, Interrupt, Clear draft, Escape). A prompt counts as sent only when the transcript shows it. Inside a dialog it never sends a digit, Tab or Shift+Tab.

>What happens to Claude when Jantar is not running?

Nothing changes. The hooks fail open: with the daemon down, Claude behaves as if they were not there. JANTAR_SKIP_HOOKS=1 turns them off for one session.

>Does Meetings record the call?

No audio, no video and no bot. After you press Start notes it keeps the call's live captions, the words Google or Microsoft already show you, and the screenshots you click. Those screenshots have not yet reached Jantar on a real call.

>What if my Mac is asleep?

Then the phone can't reach it, and medicine and reminder pings wait. Keep your phone's own alarm for anything you must not miss.

>Is it made by Anthropic?

No. Jantar is independent, and not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. It works with the files and hooks Claude Code exposes.

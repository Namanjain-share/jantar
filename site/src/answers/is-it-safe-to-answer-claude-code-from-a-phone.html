Is it safe to answer Claude Code prompts from a phone?

answered for v{{version}}{{release-on}}

A phone that can approve can run commands, so treat a paired phone like your Mac's keyboard. Jantar keeps what can reach it small: it listens only on 127.0.0.1, your phone comes in through your own Tailscale or Cloudflare tunnel, pairing is a one-time QR code, every switch is set at the Mac, and a terminal answer is typed only if the dialog is still exactly what you tapped.

What keeps it small

network
The daemon listens on 127.0.0.1 only. The phone reaches it through Tailscale Serve, private to your tailnet, or a Cloudflare Tunnel with Access in front; with Cloudflare, TLS ends at Cloudflare.
pairing
A QR code that works once, for 10 minutes. Every write carries the device's secret, and over the network every /v1 route but the pairing claim needs a paired device.
scopes
Read, control, launch and admin, in that order. Only the Mac grants launch, which starts sessions.
switches
No phone can turn a feature on, add a folder or grant itself a scope, not even an admin device.
typing
A dialog answer is typed only if the screen still shows exactly what you tapped, checked again in the same AppleScript run that types it. Rows that save a rule or switch mode take a 2-second hold.
hooks
Opt-in, and they fail open: with Jantar stopped, Claude behaves as usual.

What it can't close

  • A key pressed at the Mac in the few milliseconds before Jantar types can still come first.
  • Anything running as you on the Mac, a Claude session with Bash included, can reach Jantar's local sockets and could pair itself as a device. Treat a card approval as coming from anything running as you, not only your phone.
  • A lost phone stays paired until you sign it out at the Mac: jantar devices revoke <device>.
